# Review standards

Apply the working rules in `AGENTS.md` and the pull-request process in the `way-of-working` skill. Read `scripts/check.sh` output for mechanical gates. Review the meaning, scope, and evidence of each change, including whether recorded decisions in `docs/PPC-REG-001_Architecture_Decision_Register.md` and `docs/adr/` support it.

No additional repository-specific judgement rules are recorded here yet. Add a rule only when an observed defect justifies it, with evidence of that defect.
