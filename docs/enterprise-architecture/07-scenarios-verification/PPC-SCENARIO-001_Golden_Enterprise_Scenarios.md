## 1. Scenario portfolio

Under [ADR-0049](../../adr/0049-v1-business-use-cases.md), PPC's golden scenarios exercise the complete v1 business use-case catalog (`UC-1` through `UC-6`): Scenario A anchors `UC-1` (compressor derate) and `UC-3` (backcasting), Scenario B anchors `UC-2` (crude-quality rejection and delayed lab correction), Scenario C preserves the `PPC-001` hurricane disruption (`UC-5`), and all three feed `UC-4` (interactive economic mode swing and KPI decomposition) and `UC-6` (cross-repository coherent `WorldExportBundle` benchmark, [ADR-0053](../../adr/0053-legacy-data-quarantine-and-world-export-bundle.md)). Every golden case identifies world version, branch/fork, logical clock, public source vintage, model and EPM dependency, evidence cutoff, allowed roles, expected source records, calculation oracle and failure injections. A `ScenarioCase` has case ID, version, lifecycle status, world version, branch ID, parent checkpoint, logical clock, public source vintage, model, EPM, ICE, EDI and ECE contract pins, evidence cutoff, query channel and purpose, allowed roles, expected source records, failure injections, intervention list, expected release IDs, oracle version and access policy. The scenario artifact moves `DRAFT → REVIEWED → FROZEN`. A replay uses the frozen manifest. [VERIFY-001 §1](PPC-VERIFY-001_Verification_and_Release_Qualification.md) owns run, post-run review, failure and signed qualification states; scenario qualification records the linked verification case and run IDs. Fixture facts below are `SYNTHETIC`; interventions are `SCENARIO_ASSUMPTION`. Numerical outputs are `DERIVED` or `ESTIMATED` as stated.

## 2. Derate slice

In fixture `SCN-A-T125`, the T125 Scenario A case (`UC-1`), a `SCENARIO_ASSUMPTION` recycle-gas compressor speed/driver restriction at 08:10 UTC lowers maximum recycle $\text{H}_2$ circulation to 75% (`289.17 t/day` pure $\text{H}_2$ at $R_{\min,\text{mass}} = 0.06669\text{ kg/kg}$) under [ENG-001](../02-industrial-engineering/PPC-ENG-001_Physical_and_Numerical_Engineering.md) `EQ-05`. Applied to the `SYNTHETIC` nominal `40,000 bbl/day` VGO charge (`5,781.44 t/day` at $\text{SG}=0.910$), the `SCENARIO_ASSUMPTION` compressor-circulation cap is `30,000 bbl/day` (`4,336.08 t/day`) when quench and shared $\text{H}_2$ headroom are non-binding (or `3,750.00 t/day` $\approx 25,945\text{ bbl/day}$ under the constant-uptake `75.00 t/day` shared-$\text{H}_2$ boundary check in [PPC-REVIEW-004 §3.2](../independent-review/PPC-REVIEW-004_Proof_Obligation_and_Validation_Register.md#32-worked-example-b--world-002-compressor-derate-two-stage-retry-and-shared-h2-double-allocation-po-03-po-04)). The `SYNTHETIC` plan of `36,000 bbl/day` exceeds the `30,000 bbl/day` cap by a `DERIVED` `6,000 bbl/day`. A `SCENARIO_ASSUMPTION` request of `30,001 bbl/day` must fail. Over a `SCENARIO_ASSUMPTION` six-hour interval wholly after the restriction, the requested `9,000 bbl` and capped `7,500 bbl` differ by a `DERIVED` `1,500 bbl` of feed capacity; ULSD yield (`13,260.0 bbl/day` in the `PPC-REVIEW-002 §3` slate), liquid $\text{C}_5+$ volumetric gain (`+8.81%`), blending compliance (`EQ-08`), and commercial margin flow through `EQ-05`, `EQ-08`, and Pyomo optimization (`EQ-09`, [ADR-0048](../../adr/0048-pyomo-optimization-and-langgraph-pydantic-agents.md)). OT publishes a source measurement with tag, unit, quality and time; ICE mapping and an EDI-adopted topology identify the affected unit. The graph exposes `POTENTIAL_EXPOSURE` contracts. A separately qualified model calculates product and economic differences. The planner may compare a baseline and derate branch but cannot execute a change without policy approval. [WORLD-002 §6](../01-enterprise-world/PPC-WORLD-002_Simulation_Time_Replay_and_Branches.md) fixes planner availability of the 08:12 observation at 08:12 for this fixture. [OT-001 §4](../02-industrial-engineering/PPC-OT-001_Industrial_Information_Architecture.md) uses independent fixture `OT-DELAY-01`, with planner availability at 08:16.

Adverse and replay checks: ambiguous tag, missing engineering adoption, delayed measurement, duplicate derate message, same-time command, restart at the event frontier, sibling-branch contamination and restricted correction. Expected responses are unresolved/quarantined, one physical transition and a reproducible old decision view. Compressor quench limits, recycle purity, and commercial loss remain subject to process-engineer sign-off (`PPC-REVIEW-004 §4`).

## 3. Crude-quality rejection slice

A scaled `SYNTHETIC` `500 bbl` crude arithmetic test lot (received into **segregated holding tank `TK-101H`** rather than commingled into an active crude tank heel; real Gulf Coast pipeline batches are $\ge 50\text{ kbbl}$ and marine cargoes $\ge 300\text{ kbbl}$) arrives at 08:00 UTC and produces one logistics ticket at 08:05. A failed LIMS assay is recorded at 09:00, published at 09:20 and available to a planner at 09:30. CTRM actualization waits for a disposition. Planning considers feasible rerouting or hold; finance posts later. A duplicate ticket cannot add another 500 bbl. A source outage leaves a visible incomplete reconciliation. A correction published after a decision can create a restated analysis but cannot enter that decision's evidence. Source-record and physical truth remain distinct.

The first acceptance path checks one receipt, inventory balance, source lifecycle transitions, approved quality disposition, analytical watermark, KPI grain and action authority. Inject out-of-order assay revisions, conflicting IDs, unavailable finance, restricted evidence, crash after commit and a sibling branch. Keep old release and dossier IDs for replay.

## 4. Hurricane regression and qualification
