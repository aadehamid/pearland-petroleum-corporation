## 1. Decision and scope

Under [ADR-0040](../../adr/0040-staged-mechanistic-refinery-model-and-closed-flowsheet.md) and [ADR-0054](../../adr/0054-refinery-a-flowsheet-closure-option-a-and-design-basis.md) (Option A locked), PPC builds a **First-Principles Refinery Model** ([GLOSSARY.md](../../../GLOSSARY.md)): a conservation-exact transient mass, sulfur/hydrogen element, and energy spine across **Refinery A v1** (`180,000 bbl/day` nominal crude slate), adding mechanistic thermodynamic, kinetic, or hydraulic equations wherever they change an operational or commercial decision across `UC-1` through `UC-6` ([ADR-0049](../../adr/0049-v1-business-use-cases.md)).

Refinery A v1 locks crude receipt and segregated hold tankage (`TK-101H`), CDU plus VDU cut-point separation, FCC, hydrocracker with recycle-gas compressor, shared hydrogen and utility headers, internal bounded interfaces for **SMR, NHT, Catalytic Reformer, FCC Selective HDS, feed-dependent DHT, SRU, and steam/power** (`ADR-0054`), product blending, finished tankage, quality release, terminal `10 vol%` ethanol blending, and dispatch ([PPC-REVIEW-002 §1](../independent-review/PPC-REVIEW-002_Governing_Refinery_Equations_and_Balances.md#locked-flowsheet-closure-adr-0054-option-a)). All LSR and heavy straight-run naphtha route through NHT; all LCO, kerosene, and AGO route through DHT.

Python with SciPy/NumPy evaluates the canonical world kernel. Pyomo with HiGHS (extending to IPOPT for NLP checks) executes a **deliberately reduced Planning LP** (delta-base vectors, linear blend indices, lagged assays) kept distinct from the mechanistic world kernel so `UC-3` backcasting isolates genuine LP calibration error ([ADR-0048](../../adr/0048-pyomo-optimization-and-langgraph-pydantic-agents.md)).

## 2. Interface, ownership and units

| Contract object | Required fields and rules |

| Stream or lot | Stable material/movement IDs, direction, sending/receiving control volume, half-open interval `[start, end)`, quantity kind (SI mass `kg`/`t`, standard volume `bbl` at 60 °F / 14.696 psia, rate, energy, concentration), units, reference conditions, assay revision, provenance class, and uncertainty. |
| Evaluation request | World release, branch, checkpoint, control volume, interval, operating mode, authorized intervention, source cutoffs, opening balances, active constraints, and supported inputs. |
| Result | Run and causal IDs, interval and basis, accepted quantities, closing state, binding limits, mass/element/energy residuals, evidence links, and status (`FEASIBLE`, `INFEASIBLE`, `INSUFFICIENT_INPUT`, `OUT_OF_ENVELOPE`). |
| State commitment | [WORLD-001](../01-enterprise-world/PPC-WORLD-001_Canonical_Enterprise_World.md) owns physical state; [WORLD-002](../01-enterprise-world/PPC-WORLD-002_Simulation_Time_Replay_and_Branches.md) orders, commits, and replays transitions. Failed calculations commit no partial state. |

SI mass (`kg` or `t`) is the sole conservation basis (`EQ-01`). Convert standard liquid volumes (`bbl` at `60 °F / 14.696 psia`, API MPMS Ch. 11.1) using exact rational densities $\rho_{\text{ref}} = \text{SG}_{60/60}\cdot 999.016\text{ kg/m}^3$ and $c_{\text{bbl}} = 0.158987294928\text{ m}^3/\text{bbl}$. Never sum barrels of different densities as mass. Missing density returns `INSUFFICIENT_INPUT`.

| Tankage & transfer headers | `EQ-02` (Perry's §4) & `EQ-03` (Perry's §6 Darcy–Weisbach, Colebrook–White, Churchill 1977 transition band `2100 < Re < 4000`, ANSI/HI 9.6.7 viscous pump correction): transient mass balance with exact intra-step bound crossing $t^*$; explicit transit stock $M_{\text{transit}} \ge 0$. | Check $M_{\min} \le M_k(t) \le M_{\max}$ at every crossing (`PO-01`). | CDU + VDU separation | `EQ-04` (Riazi Ch. 2–3; Gary–Handwerk Ch. 3–4): TBP cut-point split with per-crude zero-sum overlap ($\sum_j \delta_{c,\text{overlap},j} = 0$, $\sum_j y_{c,j} = 1 - y_{c,\text{loss}}$), Watson $K_w$, and cut sulfur conservation. | Cut shifts bounded within $\pm 15^\circ\text{C}$. Reject missing fractions or nonzero overlap sum. |
| HCU & recycle compressor | `EQ-05` (GPSA §13 polytropic head $H_p$; Gary–Handwerk Ch. 8; Maples Ch. 11): mass-basis `min(...)` over mechanical cap, mass-converted recycle $\text{H}_2$-to-oil bound $(w_{\text{H2,rec}}\dot{m}_{\text{rec,max}})/R_{\min,\text{mass}}$, inter-bed quench duty bound, and shared-$\text{H}_2$ allocation $\dot{m}_{\text{H2,alloc}}/c_{\text{H2,net}}(\xi)$; explicit $P_{\text{H2}}$ purity closure; exact mass closure separating gas/acid-gas from liquid barrels. | All candidate bounds share exact SI mass-rate units (`PO-05`). Tightening compressor speed, quench, or shared $\text{H}_2$ cannot increase feasible feed. |
| FCC reactor–regenerator | `EQ-06` (Lee, Chen, Huang, & Pan 1989 4-lump model extending Weekman–Nace 1970; Gary–Handwerk Ch. 6; `ADR-0056` `G3-c`): inlet CCR split $y_D(0) = f_{\text{add}} w_{\text{CCR}}$, $y_A(0) = 1 - y_D(0)$ guaranteeing $d(\sum y)/d\tau = 0$ identically; lump splits (`70/30` LCO/slurry, `20/80` dry gas/LPG) and feed sulfur split (`45/6/22/17/10%`); coupled coke/heat balance bounded by `7.0 wt%` coke ceiling at 30k bbl/day and $T_{\text{regen,max}}$. | Mass residual $\le 10^{-9}$ for any positive CCR; energy residual $\le 10^{-4}$ relative (`PO-06`). Option A bounded interfaces locked by `ADR-0054`. |
| Blending & quality release | `EQ-08` (exact mass sulfur/density; Chevron $\text{RVP}^{1.25}$; Ethyl RT-70 RON/MON with Maples/Fahim constants `a1=0.03324, a2=0.00085, b1=0.04285, b2=0.00066, b3=-0.00632`; Wickey–Chittenden flash; Hu–Burns/Riazi cloud $T^{20}$; ASTM D975 cetane). Refinery BOB gate (`>= 84.2 / >= 88.6 (R+M)/2`, premium `<= 10%` of pool) + terminal `10 vol%` ethanol (`87 / 91`); ULSD `<= 15 ppmw` S, flash `>= 52 °C`, ASTM D613 CN `>= 40` (D4737 when D613 unavailable) **AND** (D976 CI `>= 40` **OR** D1319 aromatics `<= 35 vol%`). | Unrounded threshold check (`PO-07`). ## 4. Python-verified hand fixtures

**Tank conservation and intermediate overfill (`PO-01`).** At pinned $\text{SG}_{60/60} = 0.840$ ($\rho_{\text{ref}} = 839.173440\text{ kg/m}^3$, `133.417915 kg/bbl`), `T125` starts at `10,000 bbl` (`1,334,179.15 kg`), working cap `12,000 bbl`, `500 bbl/h` in (`66,708.96 kg/h`) and `700 bbl/h` out (`93,392.54 kg/h`) for `2 h`, closing at **9,600 bbl** (`1,280,811.99 kg`, residual `0.00 kg`). Boundary test: start at `11,800 bbl` (`1,574,331.40 kg`), hour 1 net `+400 bbl/h`, hour 2 net `-500 bbl/h`. Closing stock at `2 h` is `11,700 bbl` (`1,560,989.61 kg`), yet the tank crosses `12,000 bbl` at **0.5 h** and overflows to `12,200 bbl` (`1,627,698.57 kg`) at `1 h` unless split at $t^* = 0.5\text{ h}$.

**Mechanistic compressor derate (`EQ-05`, `EQ-07`, `PO-04`, `PO-05`).** Nominal VGO charge is `40,000 bbl/day` ($\text{SG} = 0.910 \Rightarrow 5,781.44\text{ t/day}$). Under normal `SCN-A-T125` plant operation, net $\text{H}_2$ supply is `170.00 t/day` (`165.00 t/day` allocable after `5.00 t/day` header loss; `125.00 t/day` headroom after `40.00 t/day` DHT), which covers nominal (`115.63 t/day`) and planned (`104.07 t/day`) HCU demand so shared $\text{H}_2$ is non-binding. At `08:10 UTC`, a `SCENARIO_ASSUMPTION` compressor speed/driver restriction lowers recycle $\text{H}_2$ flow to 75% (`289.17 t/day` pure $\text{H}_2$ at $R_{\min,\text{mass}} = 0.06669\text{ kg/kg}$), freezing the `SCN-A-T125` binding cap at **`30,000 bbl/day`** (`4,336.08 t/day`). Across the 70-minute window `08:00–09:10 UTC` (`WORLD-002 §6`), baseline processes **1,500 bbl** and the `08:40 UTC` child branch (`24,000 bbl/day` for the final 30 minutes) processes **1,375 bbl** (`125 bbl` difference). With `2.0 wt%` synthetic chemical $\text{H}_2$ uptake (`86.72 t/day`, `1,199.7 SCF/bbl`), total mass output is **4,422.80 t/day** (`0.00 t/day` residual) across acid gas (`66.34 t/day`), dry gas (`44.23 t/day`), LPG (`154.80 t/day`), and $\text{C}_5+$ liquids (`32,643.6 bbl/day`, `+8.81%` liquid volumetric gain; complete slate in `PPC-REVIEW-002 §3`). In the separate **`SCN-A-H2CONT`** SMR-turndown boundary test (`120.00 t/day` supply → `75.00 t/day` headroom after `40.00 t/day` DHT), shared $\text{H}_2$ overrides the compressor cap at `3,750.00 t/day` (`25,945 bbl/day`).

**FCC positive-CCR closure (`EQ-06`, `PO-06`).** With $w_{\text{CCR}} = 0.04$, $f_{\text{add}} = 0.75$ ($y_D(0) = 0.030$, $y_A(0) = 0.970$), integrating Lee, Chen, Huang, & Pan (1989) to $\tau = 1$ yields $y_A + y_B + y_C + y_D = 1.000000000000$.

**Realistic summer gasoline RVP, RT-70 octane, and mass sulfur blending (`EQ-08`, `PO-07`).** `96 vol%` conventional summer gasoline base (`6.80 psi`, $BI = 10.9809$) plus `4 vol%` $n$-butane (`52.00 psi`, $BI = 139.6382$) gives $BI_{\text{blend}} = 16.1271$ and **$\text{RVP}_{\text{blend}} = 9.25\text{ psi}$** (`+0.64 psi` above linear average `8.61 psi`, pushing a blend that appears legal under linear averaging past the `9.00 psi` EPA federal summer conventional cap, 40 CFR 1090.215). Ethyl RT-70 (`40%` Reformate `98/87`, `40%` Treated FCC Naphtha `90/79`, `15%` Treated LSR `68/66`, `5%` $n$-butane `94/90`, Maples/Fahim constants) gives $\text{RON} = 91.1960$, $\text{MON} = 81.4457$, **BOB AKI $= 86.3209$** (`+0.8709` vs linear `85.4500`, `88.99` E10 AKI). Sulfur (`T24`): `100 t` at `10 ppmw` plus $x$ tonnes at `100 ppmw` $\le 15\text{ ppmw}$ requires $x \le \mathbf{100/17\text{ t}}$ (`5.88235 t`): `5 t` gives `100/7 ppmw` (`14.286`, PASS); `6 t` gives `800/53 ppmw` (`15.094`, HOLD).

## 5. Calibration, LP and fair backcasting

1. Pin the original plan, deliberately reduced Planning LP case, assay revision, forecast, price vintage, model parameters, configuration, evidence cutoff, and time basis separately from observed and reconciled actuals.
2. Parameter ranges and tolerances are locked in [ADR-0056](../../adr/0056-identity-steward-action-thresholds-and-refinery-a-parameters.md) (`G3-c`); Hamid Adesokan signs off numeric calibration (`PO-12a`) at the `WP-1` exit gate from an independent process-engineering benchmark report, and `PO-12b` verifies measurable Planning LP calibration error at `WP-3A` exit. Never claim real plant calibration for synthetic parameters.
3. Freeze calibration before comparing operating alternatives. Hold initial state, paired exogenous RNG streams, and decision-time evidence constant across branches (`WORLD-002 §5`). Report case order and interaction residuals (`EQ-09`).
4. Record binding constraints, units, uncertainty, and feasible alternatives for every economic claim. A solver optimum outside the valid envelope returns `OUT_OF_ENVELOPE`.
