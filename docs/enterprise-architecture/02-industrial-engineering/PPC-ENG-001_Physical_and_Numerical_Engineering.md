## 1. Decision and scope

Under [ADR-0040](../../adr/0040-staged-mechanistic-refinery-model-and-closed-flowsheet.md), PPC implements a **First-Principles Refinery Model** (see [GLOSSARY.md](../../../GLOSSARY.md)): a conservation-exact transient mass, sulfur/hydrogen element, and energy spine across **Refinery A v1**, with mechanistic thermodynamic, kinetic, or hydraulic equations added wherever they change an operational or commercial decision across use cases `UC-1` through `UC-6` ([ADR-0049](../../adr/0049-v1-business-use-cases.md)).

Refinery A v1 locks the minimum physically closed flowsheet: crude receipt and tankage → CDU plus VDU (TBP cut-point separation) → FCC and Hydrocracker (HCU) → shared hydrogen and utility headers → product blending, finished tankage, quality release, and dispatch. Hydrotreating (NHT/DHT), sulfur recovery (SRU), and steam/power generation enter as explicit bounded mass, sulfur, hydrogen, and utility interface units until a scenario requires internal unit equations. Every excluded stream is declared as an assumption or boundary term.

Python with SciPy/NumPy supplies core numerical evaluation; Pyomo with HiGHS (extending to IPOPT for nonlinear blending/kinetics) owns refinery optimization ([ADR-0048](../../adr/0048-pyomo-optimization-and-langgraph-pydantic-agents.md)). Open-source process simulators such as DWSIM Core or IDAES may serve as optional independent offline comparison benchmarks, never as runtime shortcuts.

An engineering calculation proposes a transition and evidence. A failed calculation commits no partial state. |

SI mass (`kg` or `t`) is the authoritative conservation basis (`EQ-01`). Convert standard liquid volumes (`bbl` at 60 °F / 14.696 psia, API MPMS Ch. 11.1) to mass only through a declared density $\rho_{\text{ref}} = \text{SG}_{60/60}\cdot\rho_{w,60^\circ\text{F}}$ where $\text{SG}_{60/60} = 141.5 / (\text{API} + 131.5)$. Never equate barrels of different densities as conserved mass. Missing density blocks mass conversion (`INSUFFICIENT_INPUT`). Per EIA refinery accounting, hydrocracking and cracking produce volumetric gain (less-dense products occupy more barrels than heavy feed) while exact total mass (hydrocarbon feed plus net chemical $\text{H}_2$ uptake) is conserved.

## 3. Governing engineering equations and operating envelopes

Primary citations, variable tables, valid ranges, and calibration assumptions for `EQ-01`–`EQ-09` live in [PPC-REVIEW-002](../independent-review/PPC-REVIEW-002_Governing_Refinery_Equations_and_Balances.md).

| Density & reference basis | `EQ-01` (API MPMS Ch. 11.1; Riazi Ch. 2): $\text{SG}_{60/60} = 141.5/(\text{API}+131.5)$; $m = V_{\text{std}}\cdot c_{\text{bbl}}\cdot\rho_{\text{ref}}$. | Missing density or observed-vs-standard temperature mismatch fails conversion. |
| Tankage & transfer headers | `EQ-02` (Perry's §4) & `EQ-03` (Perry's §6 Darcy–Weisbach, Colebrook–White, pump affinity laws): transient mass and component balance with exact intra-step bound crossing $t^*$; explicit transit inventory $M_{\text{transit}} \ge 0$; pump head curve vs pipe friction loss. | Check $M_{\min} \le M_k(t) \le M_{\max}$ at every crossing (`PO-01`). End-of-step clipping is forbidden. Every transfer requires its matching leg or transit balance (`PO-02`). |
| CDU + VDU separation | `EQ-04` (Riazi Ch. 2–3; Gary–Handwerk Ch. 3–4): assay TBP cut-point split $y_{c,j}(T_{j-1}^{\text{cut}}, T_j^{\text{cut}})$ with overlap $\delta_{\text{overlap}}$, Watson $K_w = T_b^{1/3}/\text{SG}$, and exact cut sulfur conservation $\sum_j y_{c,j} + y_{c,\text{loss}} = 1$. | Cut-point shifts bounded within $\pm 15^\circ\text{C}$ column envelope. Reject missing cut fractions or incompatible cut definitions. |
| HCU & recycle compressor | `EQ-05` (GPSA §13 polytropic head $H_p$; Gary–Handwerk Ch. 8; Maples Ch. 11): compressor driver/speed ceiling $s_{\text{comp}}$ bounds recycle $\text{H}_2$ circulation $\dot{V}_{\text{H2,rec,max}}$; minimum $\text{H}_2$-to-oil ratio $R_{\min}$ and shared $\text{H}_2$ allocation bound feed $\dot{m}_{\text{HCU,feed}}^{\max}$; lumped conversion $\xi(T_{\text{rxr}}, P_{\text{H2}}, \text{LHSV})$ and chemical $\text{H}_2$ uptake close mass $\sum \dot{m}_p + \dot{m}_{\text{H2S}} + \dot{m}_{\text{NH3}} = \dot{m}_{\text{feed}} + \dot{m}_{\text{H2,chem}}$. | Replaces the ungrounded linear 0.75 multiplier. | FCC reactor–regenerator | `EQ-06` (Weekman–Nace 4-lump kinetics; Gary–Handwerk Ch. 6): riser yields $(y_A, y_B, y_C, y_D)$ driven by feed $K_w$/CCR and severity, coupled to reactor–regenerator coke combustion heat balance and air-blower coke-burn ceiling $\dot{m}_{\text{coke}} \le \dot{m}_{\text{coke,max}}$ and $T_{\text{regen}} \le T_{\text{regen,max}}$. | Mass residual $\le 10^{-9}$; energy residual $\le 10^{-4}$ relative (`PO-06`). No double allocation of shared $\text{H}_2$ (`PO-04`). Internal recycle cancels inside unit boundary. |
| Blending & quality release | `EQ-08` (exact mass sulfur/density; Chevron $\text{RVP}^{1.25}$ index; Ethyl RT-70 RON/MON interaction; Wickey–Chittenden flash; Hu–Burns/Riazi cloud $T^{20}$; ASTM D4737 cetane). Hindsight optimum is never scored as decision-time error. |

## 4. Hand-checkable fixtures

**Tank conservation and intermediate overfill (`PO-01`).** Baseline `T125` starts at `10,000 bbl` (`1,334,173 kg` at constant $\text{SG}=0.840$), working cap `12,000 bbl`, `500 bbl/h` in and `700 bbl/h` out for `2 h`, closing at **9,600 bbl** (`1,280,806 kg`, residual `0`). Boundary test: start at `11,800 bbl`, hour 1 net `+400 bbl/h` (`600 in / 200 out`), hour 2 net `-500 bbl/h` (`0 in / 500 out`). Closing stock after 2 hours is `11,700 bbl <= 12,000 bbl`, yet the tank hits `12,000 bbl` at **0.5 h** and overflows by `200 bbl` at hour 1 unless split at `t* = 0.5 h`. An unsplit 2-hour step is physically infeasible and must fail `PO-01`.

**Mechanistic compressor derate (`EQ-05`, `EQ-07`, `PO-04`, `PO-05`).** Nominal HCU charge is `40,000 bbl/day` VGO ($\text{SG}=0.910 \Rightarrow 5,781.4\text{ t/day}$). At `08:10 UTC`, a recycle-gas compressor speed/driver restriction lowers maximum recycle $\text{H}_2$ circulation to 75% of design at fixed $R_{\min}$, binding feasible HCU feed at **30,000 bbl/day** (`4,336.1 t/day`) against the `36,000 bbl/day` plan. At `08:40 UTC` the child branch cuts feed to `24,000 bbl/day` for 30 minutes (`WORLD-002 §6`: baseline `1,500 bbl`, child `1,375 bbl`, difference `125 bbl` feed). With `2.0 wt%` synthetic chemical $\text{H}_2$ uptake (`86.72 t/day` at `30,000 bbl/day`), exact mass leaving the HCU boundary is `4,422.8 t/day` (exhibiting $\sim +10.7\%$ volumetric gain at average product $\text{SG}\approx 0.835$). If the shared $\text{H}_2$ header only has `75 t/day` headroom after `40 t/day` committed DHT demand (`115 t/day` net supply), the shared $\text{H}_2$ bound overrides the mechanical cap and restricts HCU feed to `3,750 t/day` ($\approx 25,945\text{ bbl/day}$).

**Assay separation and conversion closure (`EQ-04`, `EQ-05`).** `60 t` crude A (0.30 light-cut mass fraction) plus `40 t` crude B (0.20 fraction) yields **26 t light cut and 74 t remainder** (lossless separation). Separately, `100 t` hydrocarbon plus `2 t` net $\text{H}_2$ entering a conversion boundary with zero holdup change requires **102 t** total outputs; an output ledger totaling `100 t` with zero declared loss has a **+2 t** closure residual and fails `PO-02`.

**Nonlinear RVP and mass sulfur blending (`EQ-08`, `PO-07`).** `40 vol%` LSR naphtha (`RVP = 11.0 psi`, $BI = 20.03$) plus `60 vol%` $n$-butane (`RVP = 52.0 psi`, $BI = 139.64$) produces $BI_{\text{blend}} = 91.80 \Rightarrow \text{RVP}_{\text{blend}} = \mathbf{37.17\text{ psi}}$ (linear volume average `35.60 psi` under-predicts by `1.57 psi`). For sulfur (`T24`): `100 t` at `10 ppmw` plus $x$ tonnes at `100 ppmw` $\le 15\text{ ppmw}$ yields $x \le \mathbf{100/17\text{ t}}$ ($\approx 5.88235\text{ t}$): `5 t` gives `100/7 ppmw` ($\approx 14.286$, PASS), while `6 t` gives `800/53 ppmw` ($\approx 15.094$, HOLD).

## 5. Calibration, LP and fair backcasting

1. Pin the original plan, Pyomo LP/NLP case, assay revision, forecast, price vintage, model parameters, physical configuration, evidence cutoff, and time basis. Keep observed and reconciled actual series separate.
2. Identify parameters with documented units and physical bounds on a declared calibration window. Flag non-identifiable coefficients; never claim real plant calibration for synthetic parameters.
3. Evaluate on a held-out disturbance window against analytical balances and published engineering benchmarks (GPSA, Riazi, Gary–Handwerk, Maples, EIA). Inspect mode bias as well as aggregate residuals.
4. Freeze calibration before comparing actions. Hold initial state, disturbance streams, and decision-time evidence constant across branches (`WORLD-002 §5`). Run calibration changes or hindsight inputs as separate cases and report case order and interaction residuals (`EQ-09`).
5. Record binding constraints, units, uncertainty, and feasible alternatives for every economic claim. A solver optimum outside the valid envelope returns `OUT_OF_ENVELOPE`.
