### Omissions and unowned responsibilities resolved

1. **LIMS and OT/historian authority** were absent from `PPC-REG-002` — now explicitly assigned above and linked to `OT-001` and `INT-001`.
2. **Cross-repo fixture publication** had no owner — now explicitly owned by PPC via `WorldExportBundle` ([ADR-0053](../../adr/0053-legacy-data-quarantine-and-world-export-bundle.md)).
3. **Legacy R2/EDI data cleanup** had no safe boundary — now quarantined as `LEGACY_PRE_WORLD_MODEL` until WP-1 passes verification ([ADR-0053](../../adr/0053-legacy-data-quarantine-and-world-export-bundle.md)).
4. **Remaining open owner/engineering assignments** (must close in WP-0 before Phase 0 code): (a) named reviewer/steward for conflicting identity mappings, (b) exact `exe.dev` VM RAM/CPU sizing for Neo4j + Postgres + NATS, (c) single-writer Delta-on-R2 lock/commit verification protocol, and (d) synthetic calibration parameter ownership and tolerances for Refinery A units.

## 3. Paper end-to-end boundary walkthrough tests

### Walkthrough A — Delayed LIMS lab correction across system boundaries (`UC-2`)

1. **08:00 UTC (Effective):** 500 bbl synthetic crude lot `LOT-44` enters Crude Tank `TK-101` (`WORLD-001` commits physical mass using custody density; tank inventory increases once).
2. **08:05 UTC (Logistics):** MySQL Logistics commits custody ticket `M1` (500 bbl) and inserts `OutboxEntry(delivery_key="log:M1:v1")` in the same DB transaction; NATS JetStream delivers `M1` to integration inbox (`INT-001`).
3. **09:00 / 09:20 / 09:30 UTC (LIMS initial result):** SQLite LIMS records failed sulfur/BS&W assay `P1` at `09:00` (referring to 08:00 sample), publishes `P1` at `09:20`, and delivers receipt `R1` to `planner/workspace` at `09:30`. `LOT-44` moves to `QUALITY_HOLD`. CTRM (PostgreSQL) holds trade actualization; physical tank mass stays present, commercial available stock excludes `LOT-44`.
4. **10:00 UTC (Planner decision):** Planner queries workspace at cutoff `10:00`. `INFO-001 §3` returns `P1` (eligible, receipt `09:30 <= 10:00`). Planner runs Pyomo LP (`ModelRun` pinned to `ReleaseManifest L1` and `P1`) and logs `DecisionDossier D1` choosing alternative crude blend.
5. **12:00 / 12:30 / 13:00 UTC (Delayed LIMS correction `P2`):** LIMS discovers lab instrument bias, records revised passing assay `P2` (superseding `P1`, ordinal 2) at `12:00`, publishes at `12:30`, delivers `R2` to planner at `13:00`.
   - **Who records original fact?** SQLite LIMS (`P1`, immutable).
   - **Who may correct it?** Only SQLite LIMS via a new immutable revision `P2` (`supersedes_publication_id = P1`), never an in-place edit or canonical-world shortcut.
   - **Who consumes revision `P2`?** Integration inbox updates Silver/Gold and emits a new `ReleaseManifest L2` (retracting nothing about historical knowledge; publishing a restated accounting view). CTRM consumes `P2` to release `LOT-44` and actualize the trade.
   - **Can the planner reproduce the 10:00 decision without seeing future evidence?** **Yes.** Replaying `DecisionDossier D1` pins cutoff `10:00`, `ReleaseManifest L1`, and `INFO-001 §3` receipt checks (`R2` at `13:00 > 10:00` is strictly ineligible; `P2` is never returned or hinted). If `P1` were also inaccessible, the query returns `UNRESOLVED` (access result), whereas an unanswered question on accessible evidence returns `UNKNOWN` (epistemic claim).

### Walkthrough B — Duplicated crude movement across transport and replay (`UC-2` / `INT-RETRY-01`)

1. **08:05 UTC:** MySQL Logistics commits movement `M1` (+200 bbl into `TK-101`, opening 1,000 bbl; concurrent 150 bbl dispatch `M2` already applied) plus `OutboxEntry(delivery_key="log:M1:v1", digest=H1)`.
2. **08:06 UTC (Crash after apply, before ack):** Consumer applies `M1` to Silver staging and writes `InboxReceipt(delivery_key="log:M1:v1", digest=H1, status="APPLIED")`, then crashes before sending NATS ack.
3. **08:07 UTC (Duplicate redelivery):** NATS JetStream redelivers `log:M1:v1` with identical digest `H1`. Consumer checks `InboxReceipt` before mutating state, sees `(log:M1:v1, H1)` already `APPLIED`, returns the original receipt, and adds **0 bbl**. Closing tank balance remains `1,000 + 200 - 150 = 1,050 bbl` (never `1,250 bbl`).
4. **08:08 UTC (Conflicting payload injection):** A buggy replay sends `log:M1:v1` with mutated payload digest `H2 != H1` (e.g. 250 bbl). Consumer rejects the mutation, routes the message to `QUARANTINED` with conflict diagnostics, leaves tank stock at `1,050 bbl`, and blocks `ReleaseManifest` publication until resolved.
5. **Analytical rebuild check:** Rebuilding Delta Gold and Neo4j from R2 raw archives replays projections only; it never invokes the canonical world engine or creates a second physical receipt.
