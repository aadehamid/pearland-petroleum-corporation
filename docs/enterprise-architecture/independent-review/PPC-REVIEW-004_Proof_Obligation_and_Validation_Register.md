## 1. Separation of assurance layers

Proving that an algorithm conserves mass does **not** prove that its transient matches a real refinery. PPC enforces four non-substitutable assurance layers ([ADR-0050](../../adr/0050-formal-verification-and-benchmark-stack.md)):

| Assurance layer | What it proves | Primary method in PPC | What it cannot prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Discrete state & protocol invariants** | Event ordering, idempotent retry, branch isolation, reader-as-of cutoffs, approval exactness, non-negative/bounded discrete transitions | **Quint** (Apalache/TLC model checker) | Floating-point accuracy or physical realism |
| **2. Exact algebraic & conservation invariants** | Mass/element closure, zero intermediate tank crossings, no double H2 allocation, unrounded blend-threshold enforcement | **Python + `z3-solver` + Hypothesis** property-based verification | Empirical kinetic/thermodynamic fidelity |
| **3. Numerical convergence & solver stability** | Step-size convergence (1 min vs 10 s vs exact analytical split), restart/checkpoint determinism, LP/NLP optimality & infeasibility diagnostics | **Analytical oracles + time-step refinement + solver KKT/residual checks** | Real refinery calibration |
| **4. Empirical & engineering credibility** | Yield envelopes, H2 uptake, coke/heat balance, volumetric gain, RVP/RON/flash/cloud trends vs published benchmarks | **Independent textbook/API/EIA benchmarks + process/controls engineer review** | Formal absence of software bugs |

## 3. Worked proof-obligation examples and expected counterexamples

### 3.1 Worked example A — `ENG-001` tank intermediate overflow despite legal closing stock (`PO-01`)

- **Fixture (`SYNTHETIC`):** Tank `TK-101` has working limits $V_{\min} = 1{,}000\text{ bbl}$, $V_{\max} = 12{,}000\text{ bbl}$, constant density $\rho = 839.17\text{ kg/m}^3$ ($133.42\text{ kg/bbl}$), opening stock $V(0) = 11{,}800\text{ bbl}$ ($1{,}574{,}324\text{ kg}$).
  - Segment 1 ($t \in [0, 1\text{ h})$): inflow $600\text{ bbl/h}$, outflow $200\text{ bbl/h}$ (net $+400\text{ bbl/h}$).
  - Segment 2 ($t \in [1, 2\text{ h})$): inflow $0\text{ bbl/h}$, outflow $500\text{ bbl/h}$ (net $-500\text{ bbl/h}$).
- **How the later verifier detects the defect:** Z3 asserts the universal path constraint $\forall t \in [0, 2]: V_{\min} \le V(t) \le V_{\max}$ over the piecewise-linear trajectory $V(t)$ rather than checking only $V(2) = 11{,}700\text{ bbl}$. Solving $11{,}800 + 400 t^* = 12{,}000$ yields the exact violation boundary $t^* = 0.5\text{ h}$, with peak unsplit stock $V(1.0) = 12{,}200\text{ bbl} > 12{,}000\text{ bbl}$ ($+200\text{ bbl}$ overflow). The verifier rejects any engine step that advances to $t=1.0$ without splitting at $t^* = 0.5\text{ h}$ and applying a declared throttle/divert/block policy.
- **Expected counterexample if the intra-step constraint is removed:** Removing the intra-step invariant and checking only end-of-horizon stock allows Z3/Quint to emit the counterexample trace `[(t=0, V=11800), (t=1, V=12200 [UNCHECKED]), (t=2, V=11700 [PASS])]` — proving that a legal closing balance ($11{,}700 \le 12{,}000$) silently hides a $200\text{ bbl}$ physical tank overflow.

### 3.2 Worked example B — `WORLD-002` compressor derate: duplicate retry and shared H2 double allocation (`PO-03`, `PO-04`)

1. **Rejecting duplicate physical effects on retry (`PO-03`):**
   - *Scenario:* At `08:10` UTC, derate event `EVT-DER-01` reduces compressor speed ceiling (capping HCU feed at `30,000 bbl/day`), and a companion movement event `EVT-MOV-01` transfers `500 bbl` (`66.71 t`) of ULSD to terminal tank `TK-201`. A network timeout causes `EVT-MOV-01` (`delivery_key="ref:MOV-01:v1"`, digest `H1`) to be redelivered at `08:11` UTC after the first commit succeeded.
   - *How Quint verifies it:* The Quint specification models the outbox, at-least-once network channel (allowing arbitrary duplication/reordering), and consumer inbox guard `if (key in applied_keys) then assert(digest == applied_digest[key]) && no_op else apply_and_record`. Apalache checks the invariant `TotalAppliedTransfers(key) == 1` across all crash/retry traces.
   - *Expected counterexample if the inbox deduplication guard is removed:* Apalache immediately produces a 3-step counterexample trace: `Commit(MOV-01)` → `Deliver(MOV-01) [applied=1, TK-201 += 500 bbl]` → `CrashBeforeAck` → `Redeliver(MOV-01) [applied=2, TK-201 += 500 bbl]`, violating mass conservation (`+500 bbl` phantom inventory).
2. **Ruling out double allocation of shared hydrogen (`PO-04`):**
   - *Scenario:* The plant hydrogen header supplies $\dot{m}_{\text{H2,supply}} = 120\text{ t/day}$ net (SMR + reformer minus $5\text{ t/day}$ header loss $= 115\text{ t/day}$ allocable). The Diesel Hydrotreater (`DHT`, committed priority 1) requires $40\text{ t/day}$, leaving $75\text{ t/day}$ headroom. Under the `08:10` compressor derate, the HCU requires $86.72\text{ t/day}$ at $30{,}000\text{ bbl/day}$ ($2.0\text{ wt}\%$ uptake).
   - *How Z3 / Quint verifies it:* Z3 enforces $\dot{m}_{\text{H2,DHT}} + \dot{m}_{\text{H2,HCU}} \le 115\text{ t/day}$ across one shared header variable. Because $40 + 86.72 = 126.72 > 115$, the H2 header tightens the HCU feed ceiling *further* below the mechanical compressor bound: $\dot{m}_{\text{HCU,feed}}^{\max} = 75 / 0.02 = 3{,}750\text{ t/day}$ ($\approx 25{,}945\text{ bbl/day}$ at $\text{SG}=0.910$), proving that utility coupling overrides an isolated mechanical cap.
   - *Expected counterexample if the shared-header constraint is removed:* Checking each unit independently against total plant supply ($115\text{ t/day}$) lets Z3 produce the infeasible state `(DHT_H2 = 40 t/day, HCU_H2 = 86.72 t/day, Total_Consumed = 126.72 > 115 = Supply)`, creating $11.72\text{ t/day}$ of nonexistent hydrogen and overstating feasible ULSD output by $\sim 4{,}055\text{ bbl/day}$ feed equivalent.

## 4. Engineering judgment boundaries (process & controls engineer sign-off)

Formal proofs cannot settle physical realism. A qualified process or controls engineer must review and sign off on these modeling assumptions before declaring Refinery A empirically credible (`PO-12`):

1. **CDU/VDU cut-point overlap (`EQ-04`):** Whether the simplified overlap term $\delta_{\text{overlap}}$ adequately represents fractionation tailing for ASTM D86 T90/T95 diesel cloud and flash limits.
2. **HCU compressor-to-severity coupling (`EQ-05`):** Whether minimum H2-to-oil ratio $R_{\min}$ and quench-gas capacity capture the true limiting constraint during partial recycle-compressor derates versus catalyst bed $\Delta T$ runaway.
3. **FCC lump parameterization (`EQ-06`):** Whether 4-lump Weekman–Nace kinetics plus feed CCR/Watson K adequately track coke make and regenerator temperature across heavy/light crude swings.
4. **Blending correlation tolerances (`EQ-08`):** Acceptable error bands for Chevron RVP ($\pm 0.3\text{ psi}$), Ethyl RT-70 RON/MON ($\pm 0.5\text{ octane}$), Wickey–Chittenden flash ($\pm 2^\circ\text{C}$), and Hu–Burns cloud point ($\pm 2^\circ\text{C}$) for synthetic Gulf Coast pools.
5. **Control-loop abstraction (`ADR-0043`):** Confirming that 1-minute quasi-steady process steps with instantaneous/ramped setpoints do not mask fast regulatory-control instability relevant to the target business use cases `UC-1`–`UC-6`.
