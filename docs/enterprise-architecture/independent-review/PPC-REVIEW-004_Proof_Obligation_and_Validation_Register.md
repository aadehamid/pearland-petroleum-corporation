## 1. Separation of assurance layers

Proving that an algorithm conserves mass does not prove that its transient matches a real refinery. PPC enforces four non-substitutable assurance layers ([ADR-0050](../../adr/0050-formal-verification-and-benchmark-stack.md)):

| Assurance layer | What it proves | Primary method in PPC | What it cannot prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Discrete state & protocol invariants** | Event ordering, two-stage atomic inbox/Delta retry, branch isolation, reader-as-of cutoffs, approval exactness | **Quint** (Apalache/TLC model checker) | Floating-point accuracy or physical realism |
| **2. Exact algebraic & conservation invariants** | Mass/element closure, zero intermediate tank crossings, no double H2 allocation, unrounded blend-threshold enforcement | **Python + `z3-solver` + Hypothesis** property-based checks | Empirical kinetic/thermodynamic fidelity |
| **3. Numerical convergence & solver stability** | Step-size convergence (`60 s / 10 s / 1 s` vs analytical splits), checkpoint determinism, LP/NLP optimality and infeasibility diagnostics | **Analytical oracles + time-step refinement + solver residual checks** | Real refinery calibration |
| **4. Empirical & engineering credibility** | Yield envelopes, H2 uptake, coke/heat balance, C5+ liquid volumetric gain, RVP/RON/flash/cloud/cetane trends vs published benchmarks | **Independent textbook/API/EIA benchmarks + process/controls engineer sign-off** | Formal absence of software bugs |

## 3. Worked proof-obligation examples and expected counterexamples

### 3.1 Worked example A — `ENG-001` tank intermediate overflow despite legal closing stock (`PO-01`)

- **Fixture (`SYNTHETIC`):** Tank `TK-101` has working limits `1,000 bbl` to `12,000 bbl`, exact pinned density $\text{SG}_{60/60} = 0.840$ ($\rho_{\text{ref}} = 839.173440\text{ kg/m}^3$, `133.417915 kg/bbl`), opening stock $V(0) = 11{,}800\text{ bbl}$ (`1,574,331.40 kg`).
  - Segment 1 ($t \in [0, 1\text{ h})$): inflow `600 bbl/h`, outflow `200 bbl/h` (net `+400 bbl/h`).
  - Segment 2 ($t \in [1, 2\text{ h})$): inflow `0 bbl/h`, outflow `500 bbl/h` (net `-500 bbl/h`).
- **How the later verifier detects the defect:** Z3 asserts the universal path constraint $\forall t \in [0, 2]: V_{\min} \le V(t) \le V_{\max}$ over the piecewise-linear trajectory rather than checking only closing stock $V(2) = 11{,}700\text{ bbl}$ (`1,560,989.61 kg`). Solving $11{,}800 + 400 t^* = 12{,}000$ yields exact crossing $t^* = 0.5\text{ h}$, with peak unsplit stock $V(1.0) = 12{,}200\text{ bbl}$ (`1,627,698.57 kg`, `+200 bbl` overflow). The verifier rejects any engine step that advances to $t = 1.0$ without splitting at $t^* = 0.5\text{ h}$.
- **Expected counterexample if the intra-step constraint is removed:** Checking only closing stock lets Z3/Quint emit `[(t=0, V=11800), (t=1, V=12200 [UNCHECKED]), (t=2, V=11700 [PASS])]`, proving a legal closing balance silently conceals a `200 bbl` overflow.

### 3.2 Worked example B — `WORLD-002` compressor derate: two-stage retry and shared H2 double allocation (`PO-03`, `PO-04`)

1. **Rejecting duplicate physical/analytical effects across two crash points (`PO-03`):**
   - *Scenario:* Movement `EVT-MOV-01` (`delivery_key="ref:MOV-01:v1"`, digest `H1`, `500 bbl` ULSD) commits in MySQL outbox and delivers via NATS. Crash Point 1 occurs after atomic Postgres Silver staging + `InboxReceipt` commit but before NATS ack; Crash Point 2 occurs after Postgres commit during R2 `delta-rs` publication.
   - *How Quint verifies it:* Quint models both the atomic Postgres staging+receipt guard and the idempotent Delta `txnAppId`/`txnVersion` write (`aws_conditional_put="etag"`). Apalache proves `TotalAppliedTransfers(key) == 1` across arbitrary crash/retry interleavings.
   - *Expected counterexample if either guard is removed:* Removing the atomic staging+receipt commit lets Apalache produce `WriteSilver(+500 bbl)` → `CrashBeforeReceiptWrite` → `Redeliver(MOV-01)` → `WriteSilver(+500 bbl)`, creating `+500 bbl` phantom inventory.
2. **Ruling out double allocation of shared hydrogen (`PO-04`, separate SMR-turndown boundary sub-case `SCN-A-H2CONT`):**
   - *Scenario:* Under `ADR-0054` (`G1-3`), "net plant $\text{H}_2$ supply" equals `SMR H2 + Reformer byproduct H2 - NHT draw - FCC HDS draw`. In normal `SCN-A-T125` operation (`107.00 + 73.00 - 5.00 - 5.00 = 170.00 t/day`), subtracting `5.00 t/day` header loss leaves `165.00 t/day` allocable (`125.00 t/day` headroom after `40.00 t/day` DHT), which covers nominal `40,000 bbl/day` (`115.63 t/day`), planned `36,000 bbl/day` (`104.07 t/day`), and compressor-derated `30,000 bbl/day` (`86.72 t/day`) so the compressor cap alone binds in `SCN-A-T125`. In the separate **`SCN-A-H2CONT`** SMR-turndown boundary sub-case, SMR drops from `107.00` to `57.00 t/day` (`57.00 + 73.00 - 5.00 - 5.00 = 120.00 t/day` net supply; `115.00 t/day` allocable; `75.00 t/day` headroom after `40.00 t/day` DHT), while the compressor-derated HCU at `30,000 bbl/day` (`4,336.08 t/day`, constant `2.0 wt%` uptake, zero-purge-loss boundary fixture) requires `86.72 t/day`.
   - *How Z3 / Quint verifies it:* Z3 enforces $\dot{m}_{\text{H2,DHT}} + \dot{m}_{\text{H2,HCU}} \le 115.00\text{ t/day}$ on one shared header variable. Because `40.00 + 86.72 = 126.72 > 115.00`, the shared header tightens HCU feed below the compressor cap to `75.00 / 0.02 = 3,750.00 t/day` (`25,945 bbl/day` at $\text{SG}=0.910$; even tighter once purge/dissolved losses and higher $\xi$ at lower LHSV are active).
   - *Expected counterexample if the shared-header constraint is removed:* Checking each unit independently against total `115.00 t/day` supply lets Z3 emit `(DHT_H2 = 40.00, HCU_H2 = 86.72, Total = 126.72 > 115.00)`, creating `11.72 t/day` of nonexistent hydrogen (`~4,055 bbl/day` overstated feed).

## 4. Engineering judgment boundaries (process & controls engineer sign-off)

Formal proofs cannot establish physical realism. Allowed parameter ranges are locked in [ADR-0054](../../adr/0054-refinery-a-flowsheet-closure-option-a-and-design-basis.md) and [ADR-0056](../../adr/0056-identity-steward-action-thresholds-and-refinery-a-parameters.md) (`G3-c`); Hamid Adesokan signs off numeric calibration (`PO-12a`) at the `WP-1` exit gate based on a benchmark report prepared by a process-engineering reviewer who did not write the kernel (`G3-d`) across these five judgment boundaries:

1. **Flowsheet H2 & octane closure ([ADR-0054](../../adr/0054-refinery-a-flowsheet-closure-option-a-and-design-basis.md), Option A locked):** Verifying that SMR, NHT, Catalytic Reformer, FCC Selective HDS, DHT, SRU, and terminal E10 ethanol outputs close all `UC-1`–`UC-6` blends inside `ADR-0056` tolerances.
2. **HCU compressor, quench, and $P_{\text{H2}}$ binding order (`EQ-05`):** Confirming whether inter-bed quench duty or $R_{\min}$ binds first during a 25% recycle-compressor derate, and validating the recycle-loop purity equation.
3. **FCC 4-lump parameterization (`EQ-06`):** Validating Lee et al. (1989) lump rate constants, inlet CCR additive coke factor $f_{\text{add}}$, and regenerator air-blower ceiling across light and heavy VGO slates.
4. **Blending correlation constants and tolerances (`EQ-08`):** Verifying Healy et al. (1959) RT-70 constants from primary tables, plus acceptable error bands for Chevron RVP ($\pm 0.3\text{ psi}$), RT-70 RON/MON ($\pm 0.5\text{ octane}$), Wickey–Chittenden flash ($\pm 2^\circ\text{C}$), and Hu–Burns cloud point ($\pm 2^\circ\text{C}$).
5. **Control-loop abstraction (`ADR-0043`):** Confirming that 1-minute quasi-steady steps with exact bound-crossing splits do not obscure fast control dynamics relevant to `UC-1`–`UC-6`.
